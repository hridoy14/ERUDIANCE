# B2B service LinkedIn strategy and growth playbook

## Policy and authenticity
Verify LinkedIn's current official rules at run time: image dimensions/formats, spam/inauthentic engagement, automation, external links, paid disclosure, content IP and account safety. Do not automate posting or engagement through unsupported bots, circumvent restrictions, scrape private data, or promise algorithm advantages. Record official URLs and date checked. If inaccessible, label POLICY_UNVERIFIED and withhold compliance claims. AI-generated educational graphics must not misrepresent fabricated data as real screenshots or research.

## Market and trend intelligence
Each research batch: at least 3 relevant current public examples if available, with publisher, permalink, date, content type, hook, format, CTA, public likes/comments only when observed, and relevance. Add buyer job-to-be-done, counterexample, source credibility, freshness and uncertainty. Trend styles are hypotheses; never copy a competitor's artwork or infer highest conversion from views. Prefer official platform guidance, credible primary studies and direct product/service evidence.

## Funnel
`Impression → useful save/share → profile or landing page visit → diagnostic/consultation interest → qualified enquiry → meeting → opportunity → customer`. Each transition needs an instrumented event or explicitly marked unknown. CTA is contextual: awareness=save/checklist, consideration=diagnostic audit, decision=consultation. For Erudience default CTA `https://erudience.com/book`, verify URL before using. UTM campaign/source/medium per post if link use authorised; do not fabricate conversion rates.

## Day-by-day 30-day plan
Week 1 buyer pain discovery (lead capture, slow ownership, follow-up audit, CRM hygiene, exception routing, weekly recap, audience comments review); Week 2 education (six-stage flow, diagnostic checklist, response SLA, escalation, handoff, measurement, recap); Week 3 solutions (implementation roadmap, automation boundaries, exception handling, human accountability, buyer objection, audit worksheet, recap); Week 4 conversion and proof (approved demonstration, process comparison, qualified CTA, FAQ, decision checklist, buyer case only with verifiable approval, recap and measurement). These are topic prompts, not mandatory daily posting; recommend cadence only after available team capacity and real analytics. Each day has its own content brief and gate chain.

## Growth experiments
A/B hypotheses: process diagram vs audit checklist; pain-led vs outcome-led hook; educational CTA vs consultation CTA; copy density; brand colour contrast. Change one primary variable when feasible. Compare comparable audience, distribution, timing and campaign context; record impressions, saves per impression, meaningful comments, qualified clicks and meetings. No promise of viral growth, ranking or lead volume.

## Review loop
Plan → source fact-check → buyer usefulness review → legal/platform safety review → creative QA → controller verdict → owner review → staged draft → separate write approval → measurement. Quarterly revise playbook using observed outcomes, not speculative claims.
